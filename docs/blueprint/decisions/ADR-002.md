# ADR-002: Parcel vs Delivery, derived business location

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-21
- Owners: Platform team

## Context

Businesses need to know where their parcels physically are. Eveider already persists two lifecycles:

- **Parcel** (`ParcelStatus`) — the item the company submitted
- **Delivery** (`DeliveryStatus`) — courier movement of that item

Collapsing “courier assigned”, “awaiting pickup”, and “in transit” into `ParcelStatus` would mix inventory with operations. Showing a business **Livraisons** nav would create a second place to understand the same parcel. Recipients as a CRM, and a `returned` parcel status, were also proposed before the operational rules exist.

## Decision

1. **The business interacts primarily with parcels. Deliveries describe how those parcels move.**
2. **Colis** is the business-facing register (recipient, current location, status, history). **Livraisons** stays an Eveider/admin operational concept. Do not add a business Livraisons section until there is a demonstrated need.
3. **Do not add `courier_assigned` (or awaiting / returned) to `ParcelStatus`.** Courier assignment remains a delivery state. For the company it is surfaced as a **derived** location.

   **Assignment exception (identity, not a Livraisons nav):** a business with `manage_couriers` may see **its own** active courier names in order to assign them on a parcel. Admin may assign any active courier (including reassignment). Courier phone numbers stay off the business Colis register. This does not add a business Livraisons section.
4. **Business location is derived** in `packages/domain` from `parcelStatus` + `pickupType` + latest `DeliveryStatus`. It is never stored. Wording adapts to pickup method; the two flows are not forced onto one identical ladder.
5. **Keep `delivered_to_locker` and `ready_for_pickup` distinct.** Arrived at the point ≠ recipient can collect.
6. **Returns, retries, and reverse movement are deferred** until the trigger is an explicit product rule. Default candidate (not implemented): uncollected after the allowed period → return to merchant. When implemented, prefer **one parcel → many deliveries** (e.g. merchant → locker, then locker → merchant) rather than an arbitrary extra terminal parcel status.
7. **Destinataires are deferred.** When added, they are a read-only projection of that company’s parcels keyed by phone. No independent create. Name stays shipment-level if the same phone is used with different names.

### Courier pickup (business progression)

```
Submitted → Awaiting courier → Courier assigned → In transit → At locker → Ready for pickup → Collected
```

### Merchant drop-off (business progression)

```
Submitted → Awaiting drop-off → In transit → At locker → Ready for pickup → Collected
```

`Submitted` is timeline origin only. Current location after create is awaiting courier or awaiting drop-off.

## Consequences

- Positive: one source of truth for “where is my parcel”; admin Livraisons stays the ops board; parcel enum stays the persisted lifecycle
- Negative: Colis UI must call the derivation instead of printing `ParcelStatus` as-is; loaders that show location must include pickup type + latest delivery
- Neutral: failed deliveries do not invent a business location — `created` falls back to awaiting_*; exceptions stay on Incidents

## Alternatives considered

1. **Widen `ParcelStatus`** with awaiting / courier_assigned / returned — rejected; mixes ops into inventory and breaks existing transitions
2. **Business Livraisons nav now** — rejected; two competing state surfaces for the same parcel
3. **Recipient CRM** — rejected; Eveider is logistics context, not the company’s CRM

## Rollout

1. Domain derivation + glossary (this ADR) — done
2. Colis list/detail communicate derived location — done
3. Returns/retries after operational rules are defined
4. Business Livraisons and Destinataires only if needed
